Klippanda Poster

A private publishing assistant for a single creator account.

Klippanda Poster is a personal automation tool. It lets one creator review and publish their own short videos to their own social media accounts from one place, with a manual approval step before anything is published.

How it works

  • The creator sends a video to their own private bot.
  • Klippanda Poster shows an approval screen with the caption, the destination account, the visibility setting and the interaction settings.
  • Nothing is published until the creator explicitly approves it on that screen.
  • On approval, the video is uploaded to the creator's own account.

Who can use it

Klippanda Poster is not a public service and has no sign-up. It runs privately for its operator and posts only to the operator's own accounts (klippanda_uk on TikTok).
