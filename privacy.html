Privacy Policy

Klippanda Poster · Effective 2026-09-24

Klippanda Poster is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the @klippanda_uk YouTube channel. It publishes the operator's own videos to the operator's own social media accounts. It has no user accounts, no sign-up, and it does not collect data about anybody other than its operator.

In short: we run no server and no database in the cloud. Everything Klippanda Poster needs is kept on one private device that only the operator controls, and it is used for one purpose only — publishing the operator's own content to the operator's own accounts.

1. What information we process

Information from TikTok

Access and refresh tokens Issued by TikTok when the operator authorises the app. They are what allow the app to upload a video on the operator's behalf.
Creator information Retrieved from TikTok's Creator Info endpoint immediately before each post: the account's display name, the visibility options the account allows, and whether comments, Duet or Stitch are disabled for that account. This is used to render an accurate approval screen.
Publishing status The status TikTok returns after an upload, so the operator can be told whether the post succeeded.

Information the operator provides

The video file The video the operator chooses to publish.
The caption and settings The description, hashtags, visibility choice, interaction settings and commercial-content disclosure the operator enters on the approval screen.
A messaging identifier The chat identifier of the operator's own private bot conversation, used only to send the approval link and the result message back to the operator.

We do not collect names, contact details, location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers or analytics of any kind. We do not process information about viewers, followers or any other TikTok user.

2. Why we process it

Solely to operate the publishing flow: to show the operator an accurate approval screen, to upload the approved video to the operator's own account with the settings the operator chose, and to report the result back to the operator. We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, training machine-learning models, or any purpose unrelated to publishing the operator's own content.

3. Where it is stored

All configuration, including the TikTok tokens, is stored locally on a single private device operated by the owner of the @klippanda_uk YouTube channel. Video files are transient: they exist on that device only for as long as the upload takes and are not archived. We operate no public server, no cloud database and no backup service, and no third party is given access to this data.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, trade or share this information. Data leaves the device only in the two directions the operator explicitly asks for:

  • To TikTok, to publish the approved video, in accordance with the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • To the messaging service the operator uses to send the video and receive the approval link.

Those services process the data under their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep it

  • Tokens are kept for as long as the integration is in use, and stop working as soon as the operator revokes access.
  • Videos and captions are not retained after the post completes.
  • Run records are pruned automatically and contain no personal data beyond what is described above.

6. Revoking access

The operator can disconnect Klippanda Poster at any time from the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions. Revoking immediately invalidates the tokens and the app can no longer post. To have any remaining locally stored data deleted, write to zeno89042@gmail.com.

7. Security

The device that runs Klippanda Poster is private and access-controlled, and the approval interface is reachable only from that device — it is not published to the internet. No credentials are embedded in any public page or repository. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but the surface here is deliberately small: one device, one operator, no public endpoint.

8. Children

Klippanda Poster is not directed at children and is not made available to anyone other than its operator, who is an adult.

9. International users

Klippanda Poster is operated from Hungary, within the European Union. Because it processes only its own operator's data, and that operator is the same person who controls the device, no transfer of other people's personal data takes place.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
